# PlaySoundPacket
- Added `Start Delay Seconds` ADO:1701203
    - When `Start Delay Seconds` is absent, playback starts when the audio is ready without advancing song time during loading
    - A positive `Start Delay Seconds` value starts the playback timeline after that delay, with audio that becomes ready later seeking forward by the time elapsed since the deadline
    - A zero `Start Delay Seconds` value starts the playback timeline immediately and enables late-loading audio to catch up without an added wait
